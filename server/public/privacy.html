Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026 · TRACE is an early MVP running on Solana Devnet.

In short: you choose to share your browsing history, we pay you in TRACE for it, and we only share insights with others after anonymizing and aggregating them. You can stop sharing at any time from the extension, and request access to or deletion of your data by email.

What we collect

Only after you accept in the extension and grant Chrome's history permission:

  • Browsing history: for each visit, the page address (URL) and title, the date and time, and how you got there (link, typed, bookmark…).
  • A random device ID generated by the extension. It does not contain your name or email.
  • Your Solana wallet address, if you link one, and the one-time codes used to verify your signature.
  • A device check, when you link a wallet. See Device check below.
  • A salted hash of your IP address, to limit abuse (how many requests and new devices come from one network). Never the IP itself; deleted after 2 days.
  • Reward records: your points and the payouts sent to you.

We never collect files on your computer, browser-internal pages, extensions, localhost or private-network addresses (these are filtered out before anything leaves your browser), nor page contents, cookies, passwords or form data.

Device check

When you link a wallet, the linking page runs Fingerprint to recognize your browser and device. It makes sure each device links one wallet only and turns away bots, virtual machines and tampered browsers.

  • What we keep: an identifier of your browser and device, the wallet it linked, and a few risk signals from that check: whether a VPN or private browsing was used, a risk score, and a hash of your network and browser setup (never your IP address itself).
  • Why: only for the security of the platform: preventing one device from claiming rewards with many wallets, and holding the payouts of suspicious accounts for manual review. Nobody is refused because of a risk score alone.
  • Sharing: never shared with or sold to anyone.
  • Retention: kept together with the wallet address it was linked to, also after a deletion request, because deleting it would let the same device claim rewards with a new wallet.

Why we use it

  • To calculate your points and pay your TRACE rewards.
  • To produce anonymized, aggregated insights about browsing trends (for example, how often categories of websites are visited), which we may share with or sell to third parties. These insights do not identify you.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse of the reward program.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give in the extension and can withdraw at any time.

Who else handles it

  • Infrastructure providers that store and serve data on our behalf: Supabase (database, hosted in Frankfurt, EU) and Vercel (hosting).
  • Fingerprint (FingerprintJS, Inc.): runs the device check when you link a wallet, on its EU servers. We do not send it your browsing history.
  • The Solana blockchain: reward payouts are public transactions. Anyone can see your wallet address and the amounts it received.
  • Third parties who receive anonymized, aggregated insights only.

How long we keep it

  • Shared browsing history: up to 18 months after we receive it, then it is deleted automatically.
  • Anonymized, aggregated data and insights: they no longer relate to you and may be kept without a time limit.
  • Reward records: kept, because the same information is permanently public on the Solana blockchain.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop sharing: withdraw consent from the extension settings at any time. No new history is collected from then on.
  • Access or deletion: email privacy@trace-rewards.vercel.app with your device ID (shown in the extension settings). We answer within 30 days. We may need to verify that the request comes from the owner of that device.
  • Deletion does not reach anonymized, aggregated insights already produced or shared, nor transactions already recorded on the Solana blockchain.
  • If you are in the EU, you can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, and show the date of the last update at the top.
