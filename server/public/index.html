TRACE

Your browsing history is already being harvested for free. TRACE pays you for the part you choose to share, in tokens on Solana.

Download the Chrome extension TRACE token on Explorer ↗

Try it in 3 minutes

  1. Install. Download and unzip the extension, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and click Load unpacked on the unzipped folder.
  2. Consent. A welcome tab explains exactly what is shared. Accept and confirm Chrome's history permission: the first sync uploads up to 90 days of history.
  3. Set Phantom to Devnet. Phantom → Settings → Developer settings → turn on Testnet mode and pick Solana Devnet.
  4. Link your wallet. In the extension popup click Link with Phantom and sign the message. Signing is free and moves no funds.
  5. Get paid. If your history has at least 7 active days, 500 TRACE land in your wallet within seconds, with a link to the transaction.

How rewards work

1,000,000,000fixed supply, mint authority revoked
500Mreward pool, paid out weekly
500 TRACEwelcome bonus, sent instantly
  • Points = unique pages (capped at 1,000) + 100 per active day (a day with at least 5 visits). Max 1,700 per week. A script that opens thousands of URLs gains nothing past the cap.
  • Weekly budget starts at 5M TRACE and shrinks 1% every week: the sum of all budgets is exactly the 500M pool, so it never runs dry.
  • Fair split: each week's budget is shared in proportion to points, capped at 1 TRACE per point. Unused budget stays in the pool.
  • Paid after the week closes, automatically, by a daily job. Every payout links to its Solana transaction.

Why Solana

Rewards are tiny and frequent: they only make sense if a transfer costs a fraction of a cent and settles in seconds. Users receive a real token they own in their own wallet, with a public, fixed supply anyone can verify, instead of points in a database we control.

Privacy by design

  • Nothing is shared without explicit consent; the history permission is optional and revocable at any time.
  • Local files, browser-internal pages, localhost and private-network addresses never leave the browser.
  • Wallets are linked by signature, and a linked wallet can only be changed by signing with it: knowing someone's device ID is not enough to redirect their rewards.
  • One click deletes all your data from the server.
