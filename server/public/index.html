Get started

Install in one minute.

TRACE is not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you load it directly into Chrome. It takes four clicks.

Download extension (.zip)
  1. Unzip trace-extension.zip.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the trace-extension folder.
  4. Accept on the welcome page. Your first sync starts right away.
  5. In Phantom, switch to Solana Devnet (Settings → Developer settings → Testnet mode), then click Link with Phantom in the TRACE popup. If you use an ad or tracker blocker, pause it on the signing page: the one-time device check needs to load.

Privacy by design

Your data, on your terms.

Browsing history is personal. TRACE is built so that sharing is always explicit, limited and fairly paid.

Consent first

Opt-in, revocable anytime

Chrome's history permission is optional and only requested when you accept. Withdraw it with one click.

Filtered locally

Private stuff stays private

Local files, browser-internal pages, localhost and private-network addresses never leave your browser.

Signature-locked

Nobody can redirect your rewards

Your wallet is linked by signature, and changing it requires a signature from that same wallet. Each visit is paid once, so the same history can't be cashed in twice.

Fairly paid

Every payout is public

What you share earns TRACE every week, and every payout is a Solana transaction anyone can verify.

On-chain

A real token with a fixed supply. Verify everything.

TRACE is a Token-2022 mint on Solana. The whole supply was minted in one transaction that also revoked the mint authority: nobody can ever create more.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is shared?

For each visit: the page address and title, the time, and how you got there (link, typed, bookmark). Plus a random device ID and, if you link one, your wallet address. Never page contents, cookies, passwords or form data.

Who sees my data?

Only TRACE and the infrastructure providers that host it. Third parties only receive anonymized, aggregated insights about browsing trends, which can't identify you. Your payouts are public on the Solana blockchain, like every transaction.

Do I need SOL or crypto experience?

No. You only need a Phantom wallet to receive tokens. TRACE pays all transaction fees, and linking your wallet is a free signature, not a transaction.

Is this real money?

Not yet. The MVP runs on Solana Devnet, so TRACE tokens are test tokens with no market value. The mechanics, transactions and fixed supply are real and verifiable on-chain.

Why Solana?

Rewards are small and frequent. They only make sense if a transfer costs a fraction of a cent and settles in seconds, and if you truly own what you earn in your own wallet.

How do I access or delete my data?

Email privacy@trace-rewards.vercel.app with the device ID shown in the extension settings, and we'll answer within 30 days. Shared history is kept for up to 18 months. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I stop sharing?

Yes. Withdraw consent from the extension settings at any time and syncing stops immediately. You keep the TRACE you earned. History you've already shared is kept for up to 18 months, unless you ask us to delete it.
